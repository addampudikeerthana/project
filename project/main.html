About Fitness Tracker

Simple fitness monitoring system

Our Project

Fitness Tracker is designed to help users maintain their daily fitness information in one place.

Users can enter their steps, calories burned, water intake and weight.

The application stores the data using browser Local Storage.

Problem Statement

Many people do not regularly track their daily fitness activities. Our Fitness Tracker provides a simple way to record and view important fitness information.
